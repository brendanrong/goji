Goji now tells you when something goes wrong, understands more of how you talk, and fits text to where your cursor is.

## What's new

- **Failures are visible.** A dead or wrong microphone, a blocked paste, a failed transcription: each shows a card at the bottom of the screen with the reason and a countdown, and mic problems include a picker to switch right there. Copy Diagnostics in the menu bar grabs everything needed to debug.
- **Spoken commands, no AI needed.** "New line", "new paragraph", "scratch that" (drops back to the previous sentence), filler words (um, uh), and stutters ("the the") are handled on every Mac, instantly.
- **Spoken punctuation.** "Comma", "full stop", "question mark", "open quote" / "close quote", "open bracket", "at sign", "dash" and friends become the marks. Nouns like "the comma" are left alone.
- **Numbers as digits.** "Eighty kilos" becomes 80 kilos, "five point one" becomes 5.1, "fifteenth" becomes 15th, "GPT six" becomes GPT 6. One to nine stay as words.
- **Text fits the caret.** Continue mid-sentence and Goji lowercases the first letter and adds the space you need; dictate before a full stop and it won't leave a stray space. Works wherever the app exposes its text field.
- **Per-app formatting.** New Apps pane: Slack gets lowercase with no trailing full stop out of the box, editors and terminals skip AI cleanup, and you can add any app with its own casing and full-stop rules. Plus a global "lowercase everything" toggle.
- **Fix Last Dictation.** Edit what Goji heard; changed words appear as rules you can keep ("pod" to "PRD", forever), and the pasted text is swapped in place.
- **Undo Last Dictation** in the menu bar.
- **AI cleanup is faster and more careful.** About 2.5x faster on short dictations, and it now only touches punctuation and capitalisation instead of rewriting your wording.
- **Homebrew:** `brew install --cask brendanrong/goji/goji`. And `Goji-with-model.dmg` for machines that can't download the speech model at first run.

## What's fixed

- The last word no longer gets clipped when you release the key as you finish speaking (200 ms of tail audio).
- Mic startup is faster (session pre-built between dictations).
- Apple Intelligence no longer drops "scratch that" corrections or merges paragraphs.

## Install

1. Download Goji.dmg below (or Goji-with-model.dmg if your Mac can't download the model at first run).
2. Open it and drag Goji to the Applications folder.
3. Launch Goji. Grant Microphone (prompt) and Accessibility (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility).

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon recommended.
