## What's new

- **Instant start.** Speak the instant you press the key and the first word used to get clipped about 40% of the time. Turn on Instant start (Settings > General > Microphone) and Goji keeps the mic open between dictations and adds the half second before your press to each take. Off by default, because macOS shows the recording indicator the whole time. Skipped automatically for Bluetooth mics, and the pre-roll is dropped when music was playing at the press so the song never ends up in your transcript.
- **Speech engine updated** to FluidAudio 0.15.6: fixes for dropped words at chunk seams and at the very end of a dictation, resumable model downloads, and a hard offline lock so the engine can never reach the network on its own. Downloads only happen when you ask for one (first run, or Settings > Models).
- **Snappier short dictations.** AI cleanup is skipped for three words or fewer ("yes", "on it"); Word replacements and spoken commands still apply.

## What's fixed

- Accidental taps are judged by how long you held the key (under 0.3 s is dropped), so a brush of the key never pastes a stray word.

## Install

1. Download Goji.dmg below (or Goji-with-model.dmg if your Mac can't download the model at first run).
2. Open it and drag Goji to the Applications folder.
3. Launch Goji. Grant Microphone (prompt) and Accessibility (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility).

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon recommended.
