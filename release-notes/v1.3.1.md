Say the link, get the link.

## What's fixed

- **Web addresses and emails.** "bolt dot new" now types `bolt.new` instead of "Bolt.dot.new", and "brendan at canva dot com" types `brendan@canva.com`. Only kicks in when the address ends in a real domain ending (.com, .io, .ai, .new, .au and friends), so "polka dot dress" stays a dress. Phrases like "sign up at canva dot com" keep their "at".
- **Card numbers, phone numbers and codes.** Four or more single digits in a row now come out as one number: "four one two three" types `4123`, and "oh four one two double three" types `041233`. Shorter runs stay as words, so "two three people" and "zero zero seven" are untouched.

## Install

1. Download Goji.dmg below (or Goji-with-model.dmg if your Mac can't download the model at first run).
2. Open it and drag Goji to the Applications folder.
3. Launch Goji. Grant Microphone (prompt) and Accessibility (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility).

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon recommended.
