Years, times, and card numbers that stay private.

## What's fixed

- **Years.** "twenty twenty six" used to come out as `26` (the stutter filter thought "twenty twenty" was a repeat). Now it's `2026`. Also "nineteen ninety nine" → `1999`, "nineteen oh five" → `1905`.
- **Times.** "three thirty pm" used to come out as `33 pm`. Now it's `3:30 pm`. "ten fifteen" → `10:15`, "six oh five am" → `6:05 am`. Amounts are left alone: "twenty thirty minutes" doesn't become a year.
- **Web addresses at the start of a sentence** keep their lowercase: `bolt.new is great`, not `Bolt.new is great`.
- **Card numbers stay out of History.** Any 13 to 19 digit number is saved to History as `•••• 2345`. It still pastes in full, and Paste Last still works for the rest of the session.

## Install

1. Download Goji.dmg below (or Goji-with-model.dmg if your Mac can't download the model at first run).
2. Open it and drag Goji to the Applications folder.
3. Launch Goji. Grant Microphone (prompt) and Accessibility (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility).

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon recommended.
